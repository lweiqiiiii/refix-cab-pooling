# Sprint Reports

One HTML report per sprint: sprint-01.html, sprint-02.html, and so on. The current report is maintained during the sprint; earlier issued reports retain their historical results.

Reports show the client and author, reporting dates, sprint targets and progress, delivery evidence, next steps, and deliverables with project pricing. They use a white background and embedded styles for standalone reading and printing.

The agreed plan and commercial terms are recorded in [Discovery's sprint plan](../01%20Discovery/sprint-plan.md). Unknown values remain unconfirmed until agreed.
